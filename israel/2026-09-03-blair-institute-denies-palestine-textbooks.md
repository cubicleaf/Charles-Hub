The Tony Blair Institute for Global Change on 3 September 2026 denied an exclusive Middle East Eye report that former UK Prime Minister Tony Blair had asked the Palestinian Authority to remove any mention of "Palestine" from school textbooks. In the original interview, Jeremy Greenstock, who served as the UK's ambassador to the UN between 1998 and 2003, told MEE that US President Donald Trump's Board of Peace had sent Blair to ask the PA to replace the word "Palestine" with the Jewish designation "Samaria" as a condition of being treated as capable of reaching an agreement with Israel over the war that began after 7 October. Greenstock said he received the information from a ministerial-level source, and that the demand was rejected by Palestinian Authority President Mahmoud Abbas.

Blair was appointed to the founding executive board of the Board of Peace for Gaza by the Trump administration in January 2026. Other board members include Trump's son-in-law Jared Kushner, Middle East special envoy Steve Witkoff, and billionaire investor Marc Rowan, who has described himself a "proud supporter of" Israel and its military. Greenstock, who defended his former boss's Iraq War-era role in securing a UN mandate before the 2003 invasion, was critical of Blair's current position, saying Blair was experiencing "difficulties" in his role overseeing Gaza's postwar management for the board and questioning "what principles the Board of Peace is working on, and what understanding they have of justice, of the sense of fairness, of the history of all of this."

After MEE's initial report, a spokesperson for Blair issued a denial, and on Thursday the Blair Institute posted, deleted, and then reposted a fuller rejection on social media: "This story is a complete fabrication. We categorically deny the claims made in a recent media report, attributed to a former British diplomat, alleging that Mr Blair asked the Palestinian Authority to remove the name 'Palestine' from Palestinian school curricula or replace it with another term. This claim is entirely false and without foundation. Mr Blair has made no such request, nor has he conveyed or endorsed any proposal to this effect." The institute said Blair remains focused on "supporting the peace process" and Gaza's reconstruction. Blair is known to have travelled to Israel in mid-August with Kushner and Board of Peace director-general Nickolay Mladenov for talks with Netanyahu before meeting a Palestinian technocratic group in Cairo alongside Hamas representatives.
