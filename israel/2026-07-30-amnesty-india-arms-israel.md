Indian state-owned and private companies shipped at least 2,596 consignments of arms, ammunition, and military components to Israel during the Gaza war, according to a new Amnesty International report, published Thursday and reported by Middle East Eye. The shipments, sent between 7 October 2023 and 30 November 2025, included machine-gun components, 155mm high-explosive artillery shells supplied to Elbit Systems, explosive warheads for SkyStriker "kamikaze" drones, and armoured-vehicle components destined for major suppliers to the Israeli military. In total, Amnesty tallied 564,970 parts of explosive ordnance, 390,516 small-arms parts, and 298 military-vehicle components sent to large Israeli defense suppliers.

Titled "Made in India: The Supply of Weapons and Ammunition to Israel," the report says its methodology was "deliberately conservative" and likely underestimates the true scale of India's contribution to Israel's military supply chain. Amnesty's researchers excluded shipments they could not conclusively classify as military, along with dual-use small-arms components. Amnesty's secretary general, Agnès Callamard, said Indian authorities "cannot credibly argue that they did not know" that continuing arms transfers carried a substantial risk of contributing to serious violations of international law, given the International Court of Justice's provisional-measures findings on a plausible risk of genocide in Gaza. "Indian companies continued to profit by supplying components and munitions destined for Israel's defence sector," she said, while the Israeli military "continued to inflict death and suffering in Gaza."

The report is the latest in a series alleging deepening Indian material support for Israel's campaign, following earlier reporting on Indian-supplied combat drones and weapons components, and a separate Al Jazeera investigation of Israeli Tax Authority data that found India was the second-largest exporter of military-related goods to Israel between October 2023 and October 2025. Amnesty said it wrote to nine Indian companies and the Indian state seeking comment but had not received a response by publication. As of 29 July 2026, Middle East Eye reported, at least 247,387 Palestinians have been killed or injured since Israel launched its Gaza campaign following the 7 October 2023 Hamas-led attacks on southern Israel.
