Israeli forces shelled the southern Lebanese town of al-Mansouri, the vicinity of Bayt al-Sayyad and the outskirts of Majdal Zoun overnight into 22 August 2026, according to Lebanon's National News Agency (NNA). Israel also carried out a separate air raid near the Ali al-Tahir heights in southern Lebanon, Quds News Network reported. NNA said Israeli drones were seen flying continuously over villages and towns across the western and central sectors of southern Lebanon during the operation.

Israeli forces also carried out extensive demolitions overnight in the affected towns, where reports indicated "no homes remained undamaged." Additional demolitions were reported in the nearby towns of Haddatha, Beit Yahoun and Aita al-Jabal.

No casualties were reported in connection with the shelling or demolitions. The incident continues a pattern of near-daily Israeli military activity in southern Lebanon that has persisted despite the ceasefire announced in mid-2026, part of a campaign that has already produced mounting civilian casualties, repeated incidents involving UNIFIL peacekeepers, and a security zone roughly 10 kilometres inside Lebanese territory.
