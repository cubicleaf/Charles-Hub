Two Democratic members of the US House of Representatives, Rashida Tlaib of Michigan and Yassamin Ansari of Arizona, publicly denounced the Trump administration's war on Iran as it passed the six-month mark, in posts on X reported by Middle East Eye on 29 August 2026. Tlaib wrote that the conflict "has claimed thousands of innocent lives, put the US and global economies on the brink, and caused suffering for millions," calling it an "illegal and immoral war on the people of Iran" and adding, "No more war."

Ansari, who separately demanded the impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, said that in the six months since the war began, 120 children died after a US military strike hit their school and 18 American servicemembers have been killed, while Americans "endured record high gas costs" and Donald Trump and his sons "profited endlessly from investing in defense tech companies that received government contracts."

The statements add to a running current of congressional criticism of the war's cost, though neither lawmaker's remarks were accompanied by a concrete legislative move such as a funding restriction or a vote to end US involvement.
