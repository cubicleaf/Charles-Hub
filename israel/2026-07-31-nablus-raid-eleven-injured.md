Israeli settlers escorted by soldiers stormed the town of Tal and other areas southwest of Nablus on 31 July 2026, in the latest escalation of the weeks-long military crackdown across the occupied West Bank, Al Jazeera reported. At least 11 Palestinians, including four children, were injured during a raid by Israeli settlers and soldiers in the Junaid neighbourhood of Nablus, medical sources said; residents said Israeli forces fired live rounds, stun grenades and tear gas canisters, and at least one of the injured was shot in the stomach, according to the Palestine Red Crescent Society. Israeli soldiers detained at least 10 people across the West Bank that day, including in Nablus, Tal, Salem and Bethlehem.

In a statement, the Israeli military said "Israeli civilians" had entered Area A of the occupied West Bank, which is prohibited under Israeli law, and that they had since left; it added that it had received reports a Palestinian cemetery had been vandalised. The raids followed a settler incursion into Tal the week before in which Palestinians were killed and several wounded, described as one of the worst such incursions in recent weeks as Israelis set Palestinian homes and vehicles on fire. Separately, on Thursday morning, Israeli forces raided Tal and seized four Palestinian homes, converting them into military barracks.

Israeli soldiers have raided communities and made arrests across the Tal and Nablus areas throughout the week, effectively imposing a blockade on the area. Separate raids on Friday hit Ramallah's Qaddura refugee camp, where forces fired tear gas and detained one person, while soldiers arrested men in Bethlehem's Wadi Shahin area and in Anabta, east of Tulkarem, and briefly detained several young men in Salfit. The night before, Israeli soldiers had shot and wounded a man in the neck when they fired on his car in Beita, south of Nablus, according to the Palestinian news agency WAFA.
