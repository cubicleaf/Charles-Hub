A Palestinian man was shot dead by Israeli forces on 23 September 2026 following what Israeli police described as a car-ramming attack that injured a soldier at a checkpoint near the illegal settlement of Beit Horon, west of Ramallah in the occupied West Bank. Israel's Ambassador to the United States, Yechiel Leiter, identified the wounded soldier as his son, writing on social media that medics "are now fighting to save the life of Neria Dov ben Chana." Shaare Zedek Hospital later said the soldier had undergone complex surgery and that his life remained at risk.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu visited the wounded soldier in hospital before departing for the United States to deliver his address to the UN General Assembly, according to his office. "My wife Sara and I, together with all the people of Israel, are praying for a miracle," Netanyahu wrote, later adding: "I recently visited the dear family of Neria Leiter... I ask you to pray for the recovery of Neria Dov ben Chana. The entire nation is with you." Leiter's eldest son, 39-year-old Moshe Yedidya, was killed while deployed in Gaza in November 2023, weeks after the start of the war.

The Palestinian Health Ministry identified the suspected attacker as 29-year-old Mahmoud Mohammad Mahmoud Suleiman and said Israeli authorities were holding his body. Israeli forces raided his home village of Beit Ur al-Tahta and searched multiple houses in its aftermath. Hazem Suleiman, the village's mayor and no relation to the suspect, said the town had been sealed off: "The town is closed off, with all its entrances blocked. There is no social or commercial activity and no signs of normal life."
