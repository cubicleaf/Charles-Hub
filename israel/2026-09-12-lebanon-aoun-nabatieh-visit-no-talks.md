Lebanese President Joseph Aoun traveled to the southern city of Nabatieh on 12 September 2026, visiting a Hezbollah position that the Israeli army had destroyed two days earlier, and said no new round of negotiations with Israel is presently underway.

According to Lebanon's state-run National News Agency, Aoun — accompanied by senior security officials — said Lebanon remains committed to "the withdrawal of Israeli forces, the return of prisoners and displaced people, and reconstruction" as "national constants."

The visit came a day after a US diplomatic official confirmed that a new round of US-brokered talks between Israel and Lebanon, originally scheduled for next week in Rome, has been postponed until October.
