Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam condemned an Israeli artillery strike on the Ali al-Taher hills in southern Lebanon on 1 August 2026 as "a blatant violation of the ceasefire." In a statement on X, Salam said the attack constituted "a flagrant breach of Lebanon's sovereignty and security, as well as the principles and rules of international law." Quds News Network had earlier reported that Israeli artillery pounded the hills; no casualties were reported in initial accounts of the strike.

The condemnation is the latest in a running series of Lebanese government protests over Israeli military activity in the country's south since the ceasefire announced in mid-2026, which Israel has continued to breach with strikes, demolitions and a roughly 10-kilometre security zone inside Lebanese territory.
