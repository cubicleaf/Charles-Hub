Israeli forces continued artillery and drone strikes across southern Lebanon on 24 September 2026, including attacks on populated areas near a restaurant in Nabatieh al-Fawqa, even as Israeli Army Radio reported a reduction in the number of ground troops Israel has deployed in occupied Lebanon in recent days. Military sources cited by Army Radio said troop rotations have seen four Israeli brigades return from Lebanon while only two new brigades were deployed to replace them, an effort described as aimed at easing the burden on a military simultaneously engaged in multiple conflicts.

The drawdown does not signal any slowdown in Israel's bombardment of the south, and Antiwar.com suggested Israel may feel it no longer needs as large a physical presence in areas it has already extensively destroyed. Former Israeli commander Oren Zini warned against the reduction, saying Israel needs to maintain "a credible hold" on the areas it occupies "or we won't be taken seriously." Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam, addressing the UN General Assembly the same week, described Lebanon's humanitarian situation as "particularly urgent" and sought international support for a crisis he said has continued to worsen for months.
