Israeli artillery shelled the vicinity of the southern Lebanese town of al-Mansouri early Saturday morning, Lebanon's National News Agency reported, according to Middle East Eye. No casualties were reported in connection with the shelling.

The strike is one of many individually reported incidents in Israel's continuing military campaign in southern Lebanon, which has produced a mounting cumulative death toll since it expanded on 2 March 2026, according to figures separately reported by Lebanon's Ministry of Public Health. The Israeli army continues to occupy parts of southern Lebanon and has kept striking villages in the area despite ongoing diplomatic talks between Beirut and Israel, talks in which Hezbollah is not a participant.
