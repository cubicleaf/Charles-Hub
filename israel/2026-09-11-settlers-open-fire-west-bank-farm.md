Israeli settlers opened fire on Palestinians at a farm east of Al-Mughayyir village, northeast of Ramallah, on Friday after residents tried to stop settlers from targeting a poultry farm, the Palestinian news agency Wafa reported, according to Middle East Eye. Marzouq Abu Naim, deputy head of the Al-Mughayyir village council, said Israeli forces then intervened by firing tear gas at the Palestinians while protecting the settlers; no injuries were reported in that incident.

In a separate incident the same day, two Palestinians from Al-Mughayyir were injured after being attacked by settlers on the Al-Ma'rajat road linking Ramallah and Jericho. Settlers who arrived in two vehicles attacked them with sticks and sharp tools, according to Wafa. The two sustained fractures and bruises and were taken for medical treatment.

Separately, Israeli forces opened fire at a vehicle in the city of Al-Bireh in the central occupied West Bank on Friday evening, causing it to overturn, Wafa reported.
