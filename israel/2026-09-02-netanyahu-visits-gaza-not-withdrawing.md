Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu visited the Israeli-occupied side of the Gaza Strip on 2 September 2026 and vowed there would be no Israeli withdrawal from the territory, continuing to reject a US-backed proposal that would pair Hamas disarmament with an IDF exit from Gaza. "We control the area. We are not withdrawing. We remain on this line. We control 60% of the Strip, and there is more to come because we are eliminating the terrorists who threaten us, we are doing that day in and day out," Netanyahu said, according to a statement from his office.

Netanyahu referenced a ground raid conducted by Israeli forces in Gaza City the previous day, which he said marked an escalation of Israel's military operations and its continued ceasefire violations. Israeli officials said the operation resulted in the capture of a senior Hamas official, and that at least four Palestinians, including two children, were killed by Israeli airstrikes during the raid. "Yesterday we took Hamas's 'Head of Shin Bet' [chief of internal security service]. We will surely learn a few things," Netanyahu said. He attributed the raid to Israel's Shin Bet security service and the IDF, though Haaretz reported that Israeli-backed Palestinian militia members were also involved — part of a network of armed Palestinian groups, including one that started as an aid-looting gang with ties to ISIS, that Israel continues to back inside the occupied side of Gaza.

Netanyahu said Israel is "doing everything to reach our goal: the disarmament of Hamas and the demilitarization of Gaza. Gaza will no longer pose a threat to Israel. This goal has actually been achieved to a large extent, and there is more work to complete — and we will complete it."

Despite Netanyahu's rejection of the disarmament-for-withdrawal proposal and President Trump's calls for Israel to stop bombing Gaza, Washington continues supporting the ongoing Israeli attacks. Israeli media reported that a 17 August meeting between Netanyahu and Trump's son-in-law Jared Kushner ended with an agreement that Israel would continue bombing Gaza.
