The United States plans to keep three aircraft carrier strike groups in the Middle East and to send an additional division of Marines, according to US officials cited on 1 October 2026 by Al Jazeera Arabic and the Jerusalem Post, as relayed by Antiwar.com.

Last month The Atlantic reported that the USS Theodore Roosevelt was preparing to deploy, with the Department of War aiming to hold two carrier strike groups in the region before the end of the year, which suggested the Roosevelt would replace the USS George H.W. Bush, in the region since April. A US official told Al Jazeera Arabic on Thursday that the Pentagon now intends to keep three groups there: the Bush will remain alongside the USS George Washington, with hopes the Roosevelt will arrive by late November. A US official speaking to the Jerusalem Post said President Donald Trump was also ordering an additional division of Marines to the region.

The report notes that the Roosevelt would be the fifth aircraft carrier used during the war against Iran that began in February, and that the strain is visible: the USS Abraham Lincoln left the Middle East in August after an extraordinarily long deployment and is returning to California.

The buildup coincides with Trump's statement in a Time interview that an attack on Iran after the midterm election was possible. Reuters reported speaking with three senior Iranian officials who said Tehran was preparing to significantly escalate the conflict if the US resumes attacks.
