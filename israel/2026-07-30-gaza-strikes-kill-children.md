Separate Israeli attacks in Gaza killed at least two children on 30 July 2026, as the IDF continued what Antiwar.com described as its constant violations of the US-backed ceasefire deal. In southern Gaza, an Israeli strike on a tent killed Rateel al-Balawi, an eight-year-old girl; her mother, also wounded in the strike, was photographed mourning her at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis. In central Gaza, an Israeli airstrike on a home in Deir el-Balah killed Zaid Muhammad Nawfal, an 18-month-old boy who, according to Drop Site News, was his mother's only surviving child after Israeli attacks had already killed her three other sons.

The deaths came a little over a month after a UN commission released a report concluding that the IDF has deliberately targeted children in Gaza and has continued to do so despite the ceasefire deal. The two children were among at least six Palestinians killed by Israeli attacks across Gaza that day; separate strikes also hit Gaza City, killing at least two more Palestinians and wounding several others in a drone strike.

Gaza's Health Ministry said in its daily update that, over the prior 24 hours, at least four people were killed by Israeli attacks, one Palestinian died of wounds from a previous strike, and a body was recovered from the rubble — bringing the cumulative death toll since the ceasefire deal was signed in October 2025 to 1,214.
