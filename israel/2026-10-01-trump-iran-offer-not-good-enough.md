President Donald Trump said in an interview with Time, released on Thursday 1 October 2026, that an Iranian proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz was "not good enough" and that renewed US bombing of Iran after the midterm elections was "possible." The remarks were reported by Antiwar.com.

Asked why he rejected the offer, Trump answered that "things that I wouldn't have approved a year ago I wouldn't have today." Asked directly whether he would ramp up bombing after the midterms, he gave a one-word reply: "possible."

According to the report, Iran sent the US a proposal last week to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and resume nuclear talks in exchange for Washington lifting its blockade, easing sanctions on Iranian oil sales, and restoring a regional ceasefire. Trump said on Sunday that he had rejected it and expected more talks that week, adding that Iran had "overplayed their hand." Iranian state media said on Monday that its New York delegation had no plans to negotiate with the United States.

Qatari officials mediated indirect talks between Trump's envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff and Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly. Axios reported that the Qatari mediators were continuing to work separately with both sides on a compromise built on the Iranian proposal. The same outlet reported on Monday that Trump had offered sanctions relief and unfrozen assets in return for nuclear concessions; Trump denied this on Truth Social, writing: "I offered them NOTHING!"
