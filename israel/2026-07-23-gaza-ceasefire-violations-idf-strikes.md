Israeli attacks across Gaza killed at least four Palestinians on 23 July 2026, according to Antiwar.com, as the Israeli military continued what the outlet described as constant violations of the US-backed ceasefire deal reached in October 2025. The Palestinian news agency WAFA reported that an Israeli airstrike on the Jabalia refugee camp in northern Gaza killed two Palestinians and wounded several others. Late the same night, a separate Israeli airstrike struck a house in Gaza City, killing two more Palestinians, according to Al Jazeera, which also reported that Israeli strikes destroyed homes in Jabalia and in the Zeitoun and Tal al-Hawa neighborhoods of Gaza City, as well as in the Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza. Photos and videos showed Palestinians, including children, being treated at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis for wounds sustained in a separate Israeli attack on a tent.

Gaza's Health Ministry, in its daily update, said the territory's death toll had risen by six over the preceding 24-hour period: at least three people killed by Israeli forces, two who died of wounds sustained in earlier attacks, and one body recovered from rubble. The ministry said that since the ceasefire agreement was signed in October 2025, the Israeli military has killed 1,185 Palestinians and wounded 3,816.

No Israeli military statement addressing the specific strikes was cited in Antiwar.com's report.
