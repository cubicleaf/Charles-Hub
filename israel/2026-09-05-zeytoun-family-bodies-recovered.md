The remains of 55 Palestinians, all belonging to a single family, have been recovered from the rubble of a building in the Zeytoun area of Gaza City, Al Jazeera reported. The victims, all members of the Malka family, had sought refuge in the building roughly two years earlier during an Israeli bombing campaign.

Gaza's Civil Defence authorities said they had spent more than 72 hours searching for the remains of more than 60 members of the Malka family. A joint funeral procession for the recovered victims is due to take place early next week.
