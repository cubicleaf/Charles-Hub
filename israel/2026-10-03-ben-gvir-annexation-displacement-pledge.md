Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir said that the entire occupied West Bank "belongs to Israel" and that, if appointed defence minister, he would work to dismantle the Palestinian Authority and push for Palestinians to leave the West Bank and Gaza, according to a Middle East Eye report published on 3 October 2026 that cites Israeli media. He made the remarks to Israel's Channel 14.

"There is no difference between Areas A, B and C. The entire land is ours," Ben Gvir said, rejecting the division set by the 1995 Oslo framework, under which Areas A and B were placed under varying degrees of Palestinian jurisdiction while Israel kept control of Area C. He claimed that 60 percent of Palestinians want to "emigrate", and said he would hold weekly discussions with all relevant parties to manage the process. The United Nations regards the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, as occupied Palestinian territory and says Israeli settlements there have "no legal validity".

Ben Gvir also called for fewer restrictions on soldiers' use of force. Asked how Israel could field more forces in the West Bank and Gaza, he said: "The more you allow soldiers to shoot and use force, the less manpower you need." He criticised Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, saying the premier had not wanted displacement enough. MEE, which describes Ben Gvir as having repeatedly made genocidal statements against Palestinians, notes the remarks come weeks before Israel's 27 October Knesset election, in which he is seeking to strengthen his position on the far right.

The report links the statements to Sukkot incursions at Muslim holy sites. On 29 September, some 966 Israeli settlers entered the Al-Aqsa Mosque courtyard in occupied East Jerusalem, following a visit by Netanyahu to the tunnels beneath it. Ben Gvir visited the Ibrahimi Mosque in Hebron the same day with hundreds of settlers; Israeli authorities had closed the mosque to Muslim worshippers during the holiday, which the Palestinian Ministry of Endowments and Religious Affairs condemned.
