Two Palestinians were killed in separate West Bank incidents on 21 August 2026, continuing a surge in violence that Palestinian officials say has now killed at least 87 Palestinians in almost 1,500 attacks by Israeli settlers and soldiers this year, against three Israelis killed by Palestinians over the same period.

A 17-year-old, identified by the Palestinian Health Ministry as Karim Sanad Raja Shalaldeh, was killed and another man wounded by Israeli settler gunfire after rocks were thrown in Sa'ir, a town north of Hebron. The teenager's father told the Associated Press at the hospital that Israeli settlers had killed his son. The Israeli military said soldiers were dispatched to Sa'ir after Palestinians threw rocks at Israeli civilians who were in the area without prior authorization, adding that some Israelis were injured and that a security official opened fire.

Separately, the Palestinian General Authority for Civil Affairs told the Health Ministry that 58-year-old Fathi Khazem was fatally shot by Israeli forces early the same day in Jenin. An Israeli army official, speaking on condition of anonymity in line with military rules, said Khazem attempted to stab an Israeli soldier during an overnight operation and that no Israeli soldiers were injured in the incident. The official said Khazem was the father of Raed Khazem, who shot and killed three people in a Tel Aviv attack in April 2022.

The two deaths add to a West Bank violence toll that has continued to climb since the Netanyahu government ordered a territory-wide military operation in late July following an attack near Havat Gilad and a deadly confrontation in the village of Tal. Roughly 3 million Palestinians live under Israeli military rule in the West Bank, alongside about 560,000 Israeli settlers.
