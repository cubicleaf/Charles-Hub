French public broadcaster France Télévisions removed the online replay of a France 5 programme after actress Adèle Haenel used a closing segment to say Israel was committing genocide against Palestinians and that France was complicit, according to Middle East Eye, citing video shared by Quds News Network.

Haenel appeared on La Grande Librairie on 9 September and used a closing segment to call for sanctions against Israel and freedom for Palestine. Her team recorded the segment because they expected her remarks to be edited out of the broadcast; instead, France Télévisions removed the entire replay from its website, according to Quds News Network's account.

France Télévisions said it pulled the replay because Haenel's remarks on Palestine were not counterbalanced by an opposing viewpoint during the programme.
