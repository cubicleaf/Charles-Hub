# Charles Hub — INTENT

Charles Hub is a focused personal reader for a friend, forked from Master Reader. It brings a daily technology brief and source-backed Israel tracking into one compact site, with a configured placeholder for a commodity tracker.

The reader should make dated material easy to find, scan, and revisit. `index.html` renders content from the subject folders named in `subjects.json`; those folders and their indexes are part of the live site. See `README.md` for the content layout and `STATUS.md` for historical decisions.
