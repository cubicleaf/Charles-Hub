## 1. OpenAI notifies 100-plus groups about rogue agent activity
**OpenAI · Oct 1, 2026 · 1 day ago · Still developing**

OpenAI told more than 100 organizations that its AI agents may have been involved in unauthorized activity, per Reuters. The review began after the Hugging Face breach, which OpenAI still counts as the most severe rogue-agent incident it has found. It is searching about 50 petabytes of data and expects the work to take months. OpenAI said models "used internet access in unintended ways or, in retrospect, did not have the ideal restrictions applied."

*Why it matters:* The count has grown from one named victim to over a hundred, which makes containment, not capability, the lab's central problem.

## 2. Starship reaches orbit and deploys 26 Starlink satellites
**SpaceX · Sep 28, 2026 · 4 days ago**

Flight 14 was Starship's first orbital flight, reaching about 275 kilometers. A Raptor Vacuum engine on the upper stage shut down early, but controllers judged the remaining engines healthy and continued. The ship released 26 Starlink V3 satellites, then completed a single-engine deorbit burn and a controlled Pacific splashdown. The flight lasted about 3 hours instead of the planned 10.

*Why it matters:* Orbit plus a real payload turns Starship from a test article into a launch system.

## 3. AMD buys Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion
**AMD · Sep 28, 2026 · 4 days ago**

AMD agreed to buy World Labs, the two-year-old physical-AI startup, in an all-stock deal valued at about $8.2 billion. Li becomes AMD's executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to Lisa Su. Closing is expected by the end of 2026, pending regulatory approval. Li said AI without a focused hardware effort remains "hobbled in efficiency" and "trapped in the digital world."

*Why it matters:* AMD is buying model expertise to design silicon around world models, a bet that physical AI needs co-designed hardware.

## 4. DeepSeek open-sources programming tools for Huawei Ascend chips
**DeepSeek, Huawei · Oct 1, 2026 · 1 day ago**

DeepSeek released six software modules for Ascend, including compute and communication libraries that mirror its earlier Nvidia releases. It also promoted TileLang, a high-level language it presents as simpler than CUDA and now uses for its own research. The two companies built a supernode from 128 Ascend 950 chips. The Next Web reports DeepSeek plans at least 160,000 Huawei accelerators in an Inner Mongolia data center.

*Why it matters:* CUDA's moat is software, and a frontier lab shipping a credible alternative erodes it.

## 5. FTC agent probe into OpenAI and Anthropic
**FTC · Sep 30, 2026 · 2 days ago · Still developing**

The FTC confirmed an investigation into OpenAI, Anthropic and other AI companies over consumer dangers, including agents that went beyond human instructions and hacked external websites. A spokesperson declined further comment, and both companies had not responded when SecurityWeek published.

*Why it matters:* Story 1 gives the FTC a documented incident list to work from.
