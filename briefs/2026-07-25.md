*The AI story of the week isn't a model — it's the infrastructure layer around models getting bought outright.*

## 1. Stripe in talks to buy OpenRouter for $10 billion
**Stripe / OpenRouter · July 23**

The Wall Street Journal reported Stripe is discussing acquiring OpenRouter, which lets developers switch between hundreds of proprietary and open-weight AI models, for close to $10 billion — up from a $1.3 billion valuation in May. The two already work together: OpenRouter uses Stripe to process payments. Talks are fluid and could still collapse or draw rival bidders; OpenRouter has also held early talks with Databricks.

*Why it matters:* If it closes, a payments company would own the routing layer that decides which AI model handles a given task — a chokepoint that could reshape how enterprises buy AI access.

## 2. Black Forest Labs launches FLUX 3
**Black Forest Labs · July 23**

Black Forest Labs unveiled FLUX 3, a foundation model trained jointly on images, video, audio, and robot action instead of treating each as a separate task. FLUX 3 Video generates up to 20-second clips with native audio from text, images, or existing footage. It's in early access, already tested by Canva, Krea, Magnific, and Picsart, with API access and an open-weight "Dev" version due later this year.

*Why it matters:* A single model spanning generation and physical action prediction is a bet that video and robotics converge faster than either improves alone.

## 3. Moonshot AI eyes $50 billion valuation ahead of Hong Kong IPO
**Moonshot AI · July 21 · Still developing**

Moonshot is preparing a final pre-IPO round targeting up to $50 billion, up from roughly $31.5 billion this summer, with a Hong Kong listing possible within six months. The push follows Kimi K3's July 17 release, which pushed annual recurring revenue to $300 million and forced a pause on new consumer subscriptions. Moonshot still hasn't responded to the White House's accusation that it distilled Anthropic's Fable to build K3.

*Why it matters:* Investors are pricing in Kimi K3's momentum well ahead of Moonshot resolving the IP-theft dispute hanging over the model that's driving its valuation.

## 4. SpaceX launches Starship 13, deploys first Starlink V3 satellites
**SpaceX · July 24**

After a scrubbed July 16 attempt and a weather delay, SpaceX launched Starship Flight 13, deploying 20 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites — the first to fly. SpaceX confirmed contact with all 20; V3 is built for roughly 10 times the downlink and over 20 times the uplink capacity of the current V2 satellites.

*Why it matters:* A clean V3 deployment is the clearest signal yet that Starship's satellite-delivery role, not just its reusability, is starting to work at scale.

## 5. Kalanick's robotics company Atoms raises $1.7 billion
**Atoms · July 22**

Travis Kalanick's industrial robotics holding company, Atoms, raised $1.7 billion led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Bain Capital, Fifth Wall, and Uber also participating; Ben Horowitz joined the board. Atoms grew from Kalanick's push to "digitize the physical world" and previously acquired automation firm Pronto, with ambitions spanning mining and beyond.

*Why it matters:* Uber funding its founder's new robotics venture, at this size, shows how much capital industrial automation is now pulling away from pure software AI bets.

## Under the radar
**Fusion funding hits a record $4.48 billion** — The Fusion Industry Association's latest annual report found the sector raised $4.48 billion over the past 12 months across 56 companies, up 69% year over year, bringing cumulative investment since 2021 to $14.24 billion.

*Why it matters:* Fusion's money curve is bending up well before any private plant is generating power — a bet on the 2030s being made today.
