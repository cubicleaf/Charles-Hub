## 1. Ninth Circuit rules AI shopping agents don't violate federal hacking law
**Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals · August 4, 2026 · 1 day ago**

The Ninth Circuit vacated an injunction blocking Perplexity's Comet browser from acting inside users' logged-in Amazon accounts, ruling it's the user, not Perplexity, who "accesses" Amazon's computers under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act. The court called the lower reading too broad, warning it "could expose users themselves to criminal liability"; Amazon's trademark claims continue separately.

*Why it matters:* First appellate read on who's liable when an AI agent acts on your behalf, and it just got easier to build agents that browse and buy for you.

## 2. SpaceX's AI unit triples revenue in first earnings report since its rocky IPO
**SpaceX · August 4, 2026 · 1 day ago**

SpaceX's first earnings as a public company: $7.8 billion in revenue, up 92% year-over-year, with its AI segment growing 247% to $2.56 billion. Net loss narrowed to $541 million from $1 billion, but $18.4 billion in capex spooked investors — shares rose over 9% in regular trading, then fell more than 8% after hours.

*Why it matters:* First real numbers behind SpaceX's AI pivot, and investors are still split on growth engine versus cash drain.

## 3. Apple demands OpenAI injunction as trade-secrets fight goes fully public
**Apple / OpenAI · August 4, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Apple asked a federal judge for a preliminary injunction and expedited discovery against OpenAI, seeking to bar it and two ex-Apple employees from using what it calls confidential product information. OpenAI responded publicly, calling the suit "careless, aggressive and oddly personal" and posting internal messages it says show Apple staff asking one of the ex-employees for help finding files after he'd left; the suit centers on OpenAI's hardware push.

*Why it matters:* An injunction would land squarely on OpenAI's hardware roadmap just as it tries to move past being software-only.

## 4. NTT and OptQC commit capital to a 1-million-qubit optical quantum computer
**NTT / OptQC · August 3, 2026 · 2 days ago**

NTT signed a capital and business alliance with OptQC, a University of Tokyo spinout, targeting a 10,000-qubit optical system by fiscal 2027 and a fault-tolerant 1-million-qubit machine by fiscal 2030. Unlike Google's and IBM's cryogenic approaches, OptQC runs on room-temperature optical entanglement, sidestepping one of quantum computing's biggest scaling bottlenecks.

*Why it matters:* Real money behind a dated roadmap to a working million-qubit machine, not another vague research MOU.

## 5. OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 prices up to 80%, says it now reaches 1 billion users
**OpenAI · August 4, 2026 · 1 day ago**

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing 80% (to $0.20 per million input tokens) and Terra pricing 20%, leaving flagship Sol untouched. CFO Sarah Friar disclosed OpenAI's models now reach over 1 billion active users and 2 million businesses, its first public claim at that scale, credited partly to GPT-5.6 Sol rewriting its own GPU kernels to cut serving costs 20%.

*Why it matters:* First hard number behind ChatGPT's usage scale, and a signal OpenAI will compete on price as Gemini and Claude close the gap.
