## 1. Qualcomm locks in up to $60 billion AWS chip deal
**Qualcomm / Amazon · Sept 8, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Qualcomm and Amazon will co-develop custom AI inference silicon and 1.6-terabit optical interconnects for AWS data centers. Amazon gets a warrant on up to 25 million Qualcomm shares at $161.26 each, vesting as it spends up to $60 billion on Qualcomm chips and networking gear through 2036; Qualcomm's stock rose more than 5%. It's Qualcomm's second hyperscaler win after Meta, and a real shot at Nvidia's server dominance.

*Why it matters:* Hyperscalers are now bankrolling a challenger's roadmap directly, a stronger signal than a purchase order.

## 2. GPT-6 Astra debuts, OpenAI calls it the "AGI era"
**OpenAI · Sept 3, 2026 · 6 days ago · Still developing**

GPT-6 Astra, trained on more than 100,000 GPUs, saturates FrontierMath Tier 4 (97.6%) and ARC-AGI-3 (99.9%) and hits 100% on ExploitBench. It's the first model OpenAI has rated "critical" under its cybersecurity framework — able to find and chain exploits with minimal human guidance — so it's rolling out gradually through OpenAI's Daybreak access program before reaching all ChatGPT and API tiers. President Greg Brockman closed the launch by saying he believes the company may have reached AGI.

*Why it matters:* A model gated for offensive-security risk by its own maker changes the calculus on how fast frontier capability should ship.

## 3. Apple unveils its first foldable iPhone under new CEO
**Apple · Sept 9, 2026 · Today**

Apple holds its "Surprise and Shine" event today at Apple Park, expected to introduce the first foldable iPhone alongside the iPhone 18 Pro line, a new Siri, and updated Apple Watch and AirPods. It's the first major launch under CEO John Ternus, who succeeded Tim Cook, and skips a new base iPhone this cycle.

*Why it matters:* A foldable form factor is Apple's biggest hardware bet in years, arriving under new leadership with something to prove.

## 4. Zero-click WeChat worm could have hit hundreds of millions
**Tencent / security researchers · Sept 8, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Researchers privately disclosed "WeWorm," a zero-click exploit that hijacks WeChat accounts via an incoming call — no tap required — and self-propagates through the victim's contacts on both iOS and Android. Experts said it could have compromised hundreds of millions of devices within hours if used maliciously. Tencent has patched the underlying vulnerability.

*Why it matters:* A worm that spreads through a phone call, not a link, sidesteps most user-awareness defenses built over a decade of phishing training.

## 5. IBM's Nighthawk r2 delivers a 25x quantum throughput jump
**IBM · Sept 3, 2026 · 6 days ago · Still developing**

IBM's new 120-qubit Nighthawk r2 uses a faster dissipative qubit-reset technique to hit over 100,000 circuits per second, a 25x jump over IBM's Heron fleet (~4,000/sec), and has already run accurate circuits with more than 7,500 gates. It's now broadly available on IBM's cloud platform.

*Why it matters:* Throughput, not qubit count, has been quantum computing's quiet bottleneck — a practical unlock for running more experiments per dollar.
