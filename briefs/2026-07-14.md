## 1. General Fusion becomes first publicly traded fusion power company
**Nasdaq (GFUZ) · July 13 · 1 day ago**

General Fusion began trading under GFUZ after a reverse merger with Spring Valley Acquisition Corp. III, and the stock jumped 40% from its $12.85 open. The deal raised roughly $150 million for a company that cut a quarter of its staff last year, and it targets energy breakeven around 2028 using magnetized-target fusion.

*Why it matters:* Fusion has been a private-market story for a decade; going public means quarterly scrutiny of the timeline, as data centers hunt for power beyond gas turbines.

## 2. Apple sues OpenAI, alleges trade-secret theft "at every level"
**N.D. Cal. federal court · Filed July 10 · Still developing**

Apple's complaint claims over 400 former employees now work at OpenAI, and names hardware chief Tang Yew Tan, a 24-year Apple veteran, as a central figure. It alleges one hire exploited an authentication bug to pull files from Apple's network storage, and that OpenAI coached departing staff on dodging exit-security checks.

*Why it matters:* The partnership that shipped ChatGPT into iOS is now a hostile-discovery fight, with named individuals, not vague competitive griping.

## 3. Chip stocks sink as Strait of Hormuz standoff spikes oil prices
**Wall Street · July 13 · 1 day ago**

Brent crude jumped 9.6% to $83.30 a barrel after the U.S. and Iran each claimed control of the Strait of Hormuz and Trump said he was reinstating a blockade with a cargo surcharge. Chip names took the brunt — Sandisk fell 13%, Intel 7%, Seagate 6% — as the Nasdaq dropped 408 points on existing AI-bubble jitters.

*Why it matters:* Chip supply chains run through shipping lanes as much as fabs; a Middle East flashpoint now shows up directly in AI infrastructure stock prices.

## 4. OpenAI ships GPT-5.6 model family and full-duplex GPT-Live voice models
**OpenAI · Launched July 11 · Still developing**

GPT-5.6 arrived as three models — Sol (flagship, tuned for science and cybersecurity), Terra (mid-cost), and Luna (fastest) — cleared for public use after government limits lifted. GPT-Live adds full-duplex voice that listens, speaks, and reasons simultaneously, and GPT-5.6 is now the default model in Microsoft 365 Copilot.

*Why it matters:* Ending the restricted rollout removes the biggest asterisk on OpenAI's releases this year, and full-duplex voice closes a real gap in how people talk to AI.

## 5. Google scraps finished Gemini 3.5 Pro base model, retargets July 17
**Google DeepMind · Reported July 13 · Unconfirmed by Google**

Reporting says Google scrapped a completed Gemini 3.5 Pro base model for a rebuild after gaps in math reasoning, SVG generation, and image quality, pushing GA to July 17. Google hasn't confirmed that date, a rumored 2-million-token context window, or a "Deep Think" reasoning mode.

*Why it matters:* Shipping a model and unshipping it is unusual — it suggests Google judged the gap to GPT-5.6 wide enough to eat the delay.

**China nets an orbital rocket booster at sea — July 10 · 4 days ago.** A Long March 10B first stage was caught by a net on a recovery ship, making China the second country ever to recover an orbital-class booster and the first by net rather than landing legs, with a refly planned before year-end.

**Meta's custom "Iris" AI chip heads to manufacturing in September — July 13 · 1 day ago.** Built with Broadcom and fabbed by TSMC, the chip cleared six weeks of testing; Meta is scaling data-center capacity toward 14 gigawatts by 2027, up from 7 in 2026, cutting Nvidia and AMD dependence.
