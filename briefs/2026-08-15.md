## 1. Apple warns iPhone users in 110 countries of mercenary spyware
**Apple · Aug 13, 2026 · 2 days ago**

Apple pushed threat notifications straight to the Lock Screen — instead of the email it's used before — warning users in 110 countries they'd been individually targeted by mercenary spyware. Apple doesn't name the vendor but has previously pointed to NSO Group's Pegasus as the archetype, and recommends enabling Lockdown Mode and, for at-risk users, contacting Access Now's Digital Security Helpline. A similar alert covered roughly 100 countries about a year earlier.

*Why it matters:* State-linked spyware keeps outrunning phone security, and Apple is now delivering the warning with the urgency of a text message.

## 2. Google launches Gemini 3.7 Flash, its cheapest coding-agent model yet
**Google · Aug 13, 2026 · 2 days ago**

Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash just three weeks after 3.6 Flash, calling it its most capable workhorse yet for coding, debugging, and agent work. Through the end of 2026 it's priced at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output — half its predecessor's launch price — and is live across AI Studio, Android Studio, Antigravity, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. The flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro remains delayed.

*Why it matters:* Google is iterating its mid-tier model faster than its top-tier model can ship, betting cheap-and-fast beats slow-and-frontier for most real work.

## 3. Uber and Pony.ai plan 2,000+ robotaxis across Europe
**Uber / Pony.ai · Aug 14, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Uber is expanding its partnership with Chinese autonomous-driving company Pony.ai to deploy more than 2,000 Level 4 robotaxis across Europe, growing out from an existing Zagreb service into four more cities in phases, plus a planned Middle East rollout. Pony.ai supplies the self-driving stack; Uber handles booking, payment, and support, while local partners own the fleets. Neither company named the additional cities or a launch date.

*Why it matters:* A Chinese robotaxi operator just got a direct on-ramp into European streets through the world's largest ride-hailing app.

## 4. Microsoft merges Copilot apps ahead of a fall "Super App"
**Microsoft · Aug 13, 2026 · 2 days ago**

Microsoft began rolling out a unified Copilot app merging its consumer app with Microsoft 365 Copilot into one experience for personal and work accounts — mobile and web this week, Windows and Mac apps in mid-September. CEO Satya Nadella has said the full "Super App" ships by quarter's end; casualties of the merge include Copilot Podcasts, Group Chat, and free Deep Research.

*Why it matters:* Microsoft is consolidating its AI surface area into one app before the harder work of making it indispensable.

## 5. Intel closes $20 billion stock sale to fund its turnaround
**Intel · Aug 12, 2026 · 3 days ago**

Intel closed an upsized public stock offering, selling roughly 210.5 million shares at $95 apiece for close to $19.7 billion in net proceeds — up from the $15 billion originally planned. Intel says the cash covers general corporate purposes, including capital spending tied to its 14A node transition.

*Why it matters:* Intel is diluting shareholders at scale to buy the capital runway its foundry turnaround needs.
