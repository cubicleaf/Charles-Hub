## 1. Nvidia's Record Quarter Meets a New Chip Rival
**Nvidia · Aug 26, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Nvidia reported fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 18% from the prior quarter and 106% year-over-year, with data center revenue alone at $89.0 billion, up 117% from a year ago. Non-GAAP EPS was $2.22 on a 75% gross margin, and the company guided to $108 billion in Q3 revenue. Supply commitments have swollen to $279 billion, mostly memory for the upcoming Vera Rubin platform, and Amazon Web Services committed to deploying 2 million more Nvidia GPUs alongside Nvidia's new Vera CPU.

*Why it matters:* Hyperscaler AI spending isn't cooling — industry capex guidance for next year already points toward $1.3 trillion.

## 2. OpenAI's First Chip Claims an Edge Over Nvidia
**OpenAI · Aug 26, 2026 · 1 day ago**

OpenAI's first custom inference chip, codenamed Jalapeño and co-developed with Broadcom and Celestica, delivers 1.5x to 1.9x more throughput and up to 4.1x faster interactive inference than Nvidia's Blackwell systems, per independent benchmarks from SemiAnalysis. The chip handles inference only, not training, and enters OpenAI's own infrastructure by year-end; OpenAI says two further generations are already in development. Analysts caution the comparison favors Jalapeño somewhat unfairly since it uses newer HBM4 memory that Blackwell lacks.

*Why it matters:* Even a partial, self-selected win narrows the case that Nvidia's silicon is irreplaceable for the AI labs now building their own chips.

## 3. Meta to Pay Up to $16.68 Billion Over Teen Safety Claims
**Meta · Aug 26, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Meta agreed to pay up to $16.68 billion settling claims from 29 states that it engineered Facebook and Instagram to be addictive to children and misrepresented platform safety. About $12.7 billion goes directly to the states; the remaining $5.3 billion is contingent on YouTube and TikTok adopting similar restrictions. Meta must impose a default two-hour daily limit and a midnight-to-6 a.m. block for under-18 users, liftable only with parental approval. Meta denies wrongdoing.

*Why it matters:* It's the largest state settlement yet over algorithmic design targeting minors, and it hands regulators a restriction template to push onto every major platform.

## 4. SoftBank Weighs a $20 Billion Bond Sale to Refinance Its OpenAI Bet
**SoftBank · Aug 26, 2026 · 1 day ago**

SoftBank is discussing a $10 billion to $20 billion bond offering, possibly as soon as September, to refinance part of the $40 billion bridge loan it took out to fund its OpenAI investment through Vision Fund 2. The loan matures in March 2027; a SoftBank spokesperson said no amount or structure has been finalized.

*Why it matters:* SoftBank is converting short-term debt into long-term bonds just to keep funding OpenAI — a sign of how much leverage now sits under the AI investment boom.

## 5. Critical Gitea Flaw Now Under Active Exploitation
**Gitea · Aug 26, 2026 · 1 day ago**

CISA added CVE-2026-60004, a critical (CVSS 9.8) remote-code-execution flaw in the self-hosted Git platform Gitea, to its known-exploited list after attackers began abusing it to deploy crypto-mining payloads. Anyone with ordinary repository write access — including self-registered accounts on default configurations — can trigger a Git merge collision that plants a malicious hook and executes shell commands as the Gitea server user. A patch shipped July 27 in version 1.27.1.

*Why it matters:* Default self-registration plus a low exploitation bar means any unpatched, internet-facing Gitea instance is an easy target right now.

## Under the radar
Amazon is shutting down Mechanical Turk after 21 years, closing the human-labeling marketplace that helped train the machine-learning era it now competes against. Separately, Reddit says it lost 86% of its ChatGPT citations after an unannounced change to OpenAI's retrieval system.

*Why it matters:* Both stories mark the same shift — infrastructure that built today's AI, whether human labelers or indexed web content, is being displaced or deprioritized by the AI it enabled.
