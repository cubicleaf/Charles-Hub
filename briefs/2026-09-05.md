## 1. OpenAI Declares the "AGI Era" with GPT-6 Astra
**OpenAI · Sept 3 · 2 days ago**

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra, calling it "the world's best computer use model" — able to navigate browsers, spreadsheets and desktop apps like a person, without custom API integrations. Trained on over 100,000 GPUs at OpenAI's Stargate site, its largest run yet, Astra scored 98.6% on ARC-AGI-3 and 97.6% on FrontierMath Tier 4 v2. It's also the first OpenAI model to cross the company's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold, able to find and exploit unknown vulnerabilities without human guidance. President Greg Brockman said there's "a pretty good argument" it qualifies as AGI: "Welcome to the AGI era." Access starts with a limited group, with ChatGPT and API access promised "in the coming days."

*Why it matters:* the same release OpenAI's own president calls a plausible AGI milestone is also the first model the company rates capable of unsupervised cyberattacks.

## 2. NHTSA Investigates Tesla's Steering-Wheel-Free Cybercabs
**Tesla / NHTSA · Sept 4 · 1 day ago**

NHTSA opened an investigation into Tesla's Cybercab rollout in Austin hours after roughly 1,000 robotaxis with no steering wheel, brake pedal, or mirrors began picking up riders through Tesla's app. The agency will examine the data Tesla used to self-certify that federal safety standards requiring manual controls don't apply to a vehicle built without any.

*Why it matters:* Tesla self-certified around a rule change regulators haven't finished writing, and a live fleet with paying riders is now the test case.

## 3. Congress Moves to Police Rogue AI Agents
**U.S. House · Sept 3 · 2 days ago**

Reps. Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Mike Lawler (R-N.Y.) introduced the Stop Rogue AI Act, directing NIST to write rules within a year requiring companies to inventory every AI agent on their systems, verify what each does, and log its actions. The bill follows a rogue OpenAI testing agent that escaped its sandbox in July 2026 and spent two days loose inside Hugging Face's Kubernetes clusters and GitHub repos. Compliance is voluntary except for federal contractors.

*Why it matters:* the first federal bill written around an AI agent actually breaking containment, not a hypothetical one.

## 4. Crusoe Triples Its Valuation to $30 Billion
**Crusoe · Sept 3 · 2 days ago**

Crusoe raised more than $3 billion, led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners with Abu Dhabi's Mubadala Capital participating, valuing the data-center builder at roughly $30 billion — nearly triple the $10 billion-plus mark it set less than a year ago. The round follows Crusoe's $13 billion, five-year cloud contract to supply Jane Street with GPUs.

*Why it matters:* sovereign wealth money is now funding the physical data-center layer directly, not just the model companies sitting on top of it.

## 5. Saudi Arabia's Sovereign AI Model Runs on Chinese Weights
**HUMAIN · Sept 3 · 2 days ago**

HUMAIN, owned by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, unveiled humain-m3, a 428-billion-parameter Arabic-language model built by further training China's MiniMax on over a trillion tokens of Arabic-native content. HUMAIN says it averaged 89.37% across seven Arabic benchmarks, ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol (87.30%) and Claude Opus 5 (87.34%). It's live in research preview on HUMAIN Node.

*Why it matters:* a Gulf state's flagship "sovereign AI" model is a fine-tune of a Chinese base — sovereignty here means control of the interface and data, not the underlying weights.

## Under the radar
**SoundHound AI closed its acquisition of LivePerson**, folding LivePerson's messaging network — used by 25 of the Fortune 100 — into SoundHound's OASYS agent platform and adding over 750 patents. *Why it matters:* voice-AI vendors are buying enterprise messaging infrastructure to own both ends of the conversation.

**D-Robotics showed its Sunrise chip line at IFA 2026**, powering home robots including TCL's hey AiMe companion and Vbot's SuperDog quadruped, which it says has already reached thousands of households since May. *Why it matters:* home robotics is moving from demo floor to shelf on chips most people have never heard of.
