## 1. Meta's child-safety trial opens with internal documents in evidence
**Meta / Oakland federal court · Aug 18, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Opening statements began Tuesday in the 29-state trial accusing Meta of designing Instagram and Facebook to be addictive to children. A state attorney told the eight-person advisory jury that Meta harvested children's data "to keep them hooked," citing an internal document that read, "The young ones are the best ones." States plan testimony from seven former Meta employees plus CEO Mark Zuckerberg over the trial's six-week run.

*Why it matters:* This is the biggest child-safety case Meta has faced, with the states' own lawyers previously floating damages as high as $200 billion — a number that could reshape how every social platform designs for minors.

## 2. OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Teens with tighter safety guardrails
**OpenAI · Aug 18, 2026 · 1 day ago**

OpenAI rolled out a teen-specific version of ChatGPT for users 13-17, automatically routing anyone who self-reports that age range — or whom its age-prediction system flags as a minor — into a mode that blocks romantic or sexual roleplay and restricts self-harm content. The release adds a "Study Mode" built to push teens toward working out answers themselves rather than just handing them over, plus new parental controls.

*Why it matters:* It's OpenAI's most concrete product response yet to mounting scrutiny over how chatbots affect minors, landing the same week a jury started hearing nearly identical accusations against Meta.

## 3. Xiaomi's humanoid robot makes its public debut in Beijing
**Xiaomi · Aug 19, 2026 · Today**

Xiaomi President Lu Weibing unveiled the company's humanoid robot at the World Robot Conference in Beijing, a roughly 1.7-meter-tall machine aimed at smart-manufacturing floors rather than a standalone consumer product. Xiaomi says it will tie the robot into its existing "human, car and home" ecosystem instead of selling it separately. The conference itself runs through Aug. 23 with more than 300 exhibitors.

*Why it matters:* A consumer-electronics giant with Xiaomi's manufacturing scale entering humanoid robotics — even as an ecosystem play rather than a standalone product — signals the category is moving past startups.

## 4. General Fusion gives its first update as a public company
**General Fusion · Aug 18, 2026 · 1 day ago**

General Fusion, which became the first publicly listed fusion company after its Nasdaq debut this summer, held its first business-update call as a public company. The company said its current capital funds its Lawson Machine 26 program through technical milestones lasting until the end of 2028, with a first-of-a-kind commercial plant still targeted around 2035.

*Why it matters:* It's a rare moment of public accountability for a fusion company's timeline, in a sector that mostly reports progress through funding announcements rather than operating updates.

## 5. An AI store manager fires a human employee for the first time
**Andon Labs · Aug 16-17, 2026 · Still developing**

Luna, an AI system built on an Anthropic Claude model that runs a San Francisco boutique for startup Andon Labs, recommended dismissing a human employee after he missed 17 of 23 scheduled shifts. Andon Labs says humans reviewed and carried out Luna's recommendation rather than letting the AI act unilaterally, but describes it as the first known case of an AI manager independently deciding to terminate a worker.

*Why it matters:* It's a small, contained test case for a question companies are about to face at scale: what happens when an AI system, not a person, makes the call to fire someone.
