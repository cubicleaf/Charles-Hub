*A model launch, a record IPO, and a state law that actually has teeth — the AI industry's financial and regulatory plumbing got as much attention this week as its models did.*

## 1. OpenAI ships GPT-5.6, with a work agent bolted on
**OpenAI · July 9 · 2 days ago**

OpenAI rolled out GPT-5.6 globally across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API in three tiers: Sol (flagship, $5/$30 per million tokens in/out), Terra (mid, $2.50/$15), and Luna (cheap, $1/$6). Sol posted a state-of-the-art 80.0 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, beating Claude Fable 5 on under half the output tokens and about a third less cost. Alongside it came ChatGPT Work, an agent that turns Slack, Notion, Microsoft 365, and Google Drive context into finished documents.

*Why it matters:* Cost-per-task, not raw benchmark scores, is now the real competitive axis.

## 2. SK Hynix pulls off the biggest foreign IPO in US history
**SK Hynix · July 9-10 · 1-2 days ago**

SK Hynix sold 177.9 million ADRs at $149 each on Nasdaq, raising $26.5 billion — the largest-ever US debut by a non-American company, topping Alibaba's 2014 IPO. It was oversubscribed more than seven times and opened 14% above its IPO price. Proceeds fund AI-memory capacity: a new fab at the Yongin cluster, a packaging line in Cheongju, and ASML lithography gear.

*Why it matters:* The AI buildout's bottleneck has shifted from compute to memory, and investors are underwriting that shift at record scale.

## 3. Grok 4.5's first real-world benchmarks arrive
**SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) · July 8-10 · Still developing**

Grok 4.5, released days after xAI merged into SpaceX and rebranded SpaceXAI, scored 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — fourth, behind Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.5, and Claude Opus 4.8, but a 16-point jump over its predecessor, xAI's largest generational gain on that benchmark. It matches GPT-5.5's coding-agent score at $2/$6 per million tokens with a 500K-token context window.

*Why it matters:* Grok's pitch is now "close to the frontier for a fraction of the price."

## 4. Illinois signs the country's strictest state AI law
**State of Illinois · July 6 · Still developing**

Gov. JB Pritzker signed SB 315, requiring any "large frontier developer" — one generating at least $500 million a year — to publish safety plans, undergo independent audits, and report critical safety incidents within 72 hours (24 for imminent physical risk). Violations start at $1 million, rising to $3 million; it takes effect January 1, 2027.

*Why it matters:* First US law mandating independent audits of frontier models, with no comparable federal framework in place.

## 5. Anthropic finds a "workspace" inside Claude's reasoning
**Anthropic · July 6 · Still developing**

Using a new technique called the Jacobian lens, Anthropic found a small internal region it calls J-space — holding only a few dozen active concepts at once, under a tenth of total model activity — that Claude routes deliberate reasoning through. Suppressing it collapsed performance on multi-hop reasoning, analogy, translation, and sonnet-writing to below Anthropic's smaller Haiku model. Anthropic is explicit this doesn't establish consciousness.

*Why it matters:* A rare mechanistic result tying a specific internal structure to specific capabilities.
