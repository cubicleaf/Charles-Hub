## 1. Nvidia to back up to $105 billion for OpenAI's Ohio data center
**Nvidia / OpenAI · Aug 17, 2026 · 1 day ago**

Nvidia agreed to back as much as $105 billion in financing for a new OpenAI data center campus in Ohio, plus a separate $1.5 billion investment in SB Energy, the SoftBank unit building and owning the site. The complex, called the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus, starts at 4.25 gigawatts with room to expand to 8, running roughly 1.5 million Nvidia GPUs that OpenAI will lease exclusively for 20 years. CEO Jensen Huang said the deal mirrors ordinary supply-chain financing, not a blank check for OpenAI's obligations.

*Why it matters:* It's the most literal version yet of AI's "circular financing" concern — the chipmaker is now bankrolling the data center that exists to buy its own chips.

## 2. Higgsfield's valuation quadruples to $5.4 billion in eight months
**Higgsfield · Aug 17, 2026 · 1 day ago**

AI video-generation startup Higgsfield raised a $400 million Series B led by DST Global, taking its valuation to $5.4 billion — up from $1.3 billion eight months earlier. The two-year-old company says annualized revenue has climbed to $700 million, with more than 30 million users and roughly 390 Fortune 500 clients on its Cinema Studio and Marketing Studio products.

*Why it matters:* A narrow AI-video tool jumping fourfold in valuation in under a year shows capital is still chasing specific AI applications hard, not just the handful of frontier labs.

## 3. Microsoft patches 421 flaws, including a zero-day under active attack
**Microsoft · Aug 11, 2026 · 7 days ago · Still developing**

Microsoft's August Patch Tuesday fixed 421 CVEs, including CVE-2026-68820, a Windows kernel-driver flaw in the Ancillary Function Driver for WinSock (afd.sys) that attackers are already exploiting to escalate to SYSTEM privileges. Past afd.sys zero-days were used by North Korea's Lazarus group, though no attacker is confirmed here.

*Why it matters:* A kernel-level bug already under active exploitation means unpatched Windows machines stay a live target while the update rolls out across enterprises.

## 4. States take Meta to trial in California over child safety
**Meta / California federal court · Aug 18, 2026**

Opening arguments began Tuesday in an Oakland federal court where attorneys general from California, Colorado, Kentucky and New Jersey accuse Meta of designing Instagram and Facebook features that knowingly hook children, and of collecting data on under-13 users without parental consent. States could in theory seek up to $1.4 trillion in damages, though their own lawyers told the judge $200 billion is more realistic; 25 more states have similar suits pending.

*Why it matters:* It's the biggest child-safety trial Meta has faced yet, and a loss could force real changes to how Instagram and Facebook are built for minors.

## 5. China returns to flight after a Long March rocket explosion
**China / CNSA · Aug 16, 2026 · 2 days ago · Still developing**

A Long March 7A rocket carrying the ChinaSat 4B satellite exploded roughly 85 seconds after liftoff from Wenchang on Aug. 10. Six days later, China resumed launches with a same-day double header — a Long March 12 carrying 24 internet-beaming satellites, followed by a Long March 2C — both succeeding.

*Why it matters:* A fast return to flight after a rare failure suggests China isn't easing its packed launch schedule even as the explosion raises questions about how aggressively missions are being queued.
