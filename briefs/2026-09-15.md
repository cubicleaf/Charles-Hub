## 1. AI slowdown warning triggers global stock selloff
**Anthropic / OpenAI / xAI · Sept 14, 2026 · Still developing**

Two days after Dario Amodei's essay urging labs to slow capability gains — cosigned by Altman, Musk, and Hassabis — the warning hit markets Monday. The Nasdaq 100 fell as much as 1.8% intraday, Nvidia dropped 3.4%, Arm fell 9.7%, SoftBank sank nearly 11%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index shed 5.8%. Altman also confirmed OpenAI won't go public in 2026; Trump dismissed the warnings as a "HOAX," saying AI "will not be stopped."

*Why it matters:* CEOs racing hardest for AI now publicly agree it should slow down, and markets are treating that as material, not just an op-ed.

## 2. Anthropic tells investors it's profitable for a second straight quarter
**Anthropic · Sept 14, 2026**

Anthropic told investors its adjusted operating income was positive for a second straight quarter, with Q2 revenue up roughly 14-fold year-over-year to $11.5 billion and gross margins above 80% before Amazon's distribution cut. The disclosure, first reported by the Financial Times, comes as the company eyes a future IPO.

*Why it matters:* Profiting at scale undercuts the idea that racing ahead requires endless cash burn — the opposite of the slowdown Amodei wants.

## 3. SpaceX hits Falcon's 700th flight as Starship's orbital debut slips again
**SpaceX · Sept 13–15, 2026**

SpaceX completed its 700th Falcon-family launch Sunday, lofting the final three O3b mPower satellites for SES — 687 Falcon 9s and 13 Falcon Heavies to date. Separately, Starship Flight 14, the vehicle's first full orbital test and first flight carrying a revenue payload (roughly 20 Starlink V3 satellites), slipped from September 18 to no earlier than September 22.

*Why it matters:* One number reflects two decades of reliability; the other shows SpaceX still debugging the rocket meant to replace Falcon.

## 4. Mistral raises record €3 billion to build sovereign European AI
**Mistral AI · Sept 8, 2026 · Still developing**

French lab Mistral closed a €3 billion Series D led by Samsung Electronics, valuing it above €21 billion — the largest equity round it says any European tech company has raised. The funds go toward compute and infrastructure, with Mistral targeting 1 gigawatt of European-controlled AI capacity by 2030.

*Why it matters:* As Washington and Silicon Valley argue over pacing AI, Europe is funding an alternative it fully controls.

## 5. LG denies TVs spy on users after standby-mode flaws surface
**LG · Sept 7–9, 2026 · Still developing**

Researchers disclosed flaws letting attackers listen in even while LG TVs sit in standby mode, part of a broader investigation alleging the sets collect more audio and viewing data than disclosed. LG denied continuously logging or uploading conversations, confirming only that its TVs scan local networks for standard connectivity; the flaws remain under embargo, patches pending.

*Why it matters:* Smart TVs sit on home networks with microphones and constant connectivity — unresolved firmware flaws are a bigger household security risk than most people budget for.
