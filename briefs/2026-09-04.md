## 1. Nvidia Buys Hugging Face for $12.93 Billion
**Nvidia · Sept 3 · 1 day ago**

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face — the platform hosting more than 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and 1 million applications for 18 million developers — for $12.93 billion, its second-largest acquisition on record. CEO Jensen Huang said Nvidia compute "will not be required" to use the platform. Hugging Face had turned down a $500 million Nvidia offer in 2025 while generating roughly $150 million in annualized revenue.

*Why it matters:* the company that decides how the AI ecosystem shares and discovers models is now owned by the industry's dominant chipmaker.

## 2. Moonshot AI Targets $50 Billion Valuation Ahead of a Hong Kong IPO
**Moonshot AI · Sept 3 · 1 day ago**

Moonshot AI, the Beijing-based maker of the Kimi assistant, is raising a round at roughly $50 billion — up from about $30 billion last month — while confidentially filing for a Hong Kong IPO targeting around $3 billion. It still trails rivals DeepSeek (~$74 billion) and Z.AI (~$66 billion).

*Why it matters:* Chinese AI labs are racing toward public markets faster than their US counterparts, driven by Kimi K3's July release.

## 3. Broadcom's AI Chip Revenue Jumps 221% to $16.7 Billion
**Broadcom · Sept 2 · 2 days ago**

Broadcom's AI semiconductor revenue rose 221% year-over-year to $16.7 billion last quarter, its 14th straight quarter of AI-driven growth, on record total revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86%. Guidance for next quarter: $21.7 billion in AI chip revenue, 236% growth.

*Why it matters:* custom AI accelerator demand is still accelerating, not plateauing, a full year into the infrastructure buildout.

## 4. A Researcher Scraped 4.5 Billion TikTok Videos onto Hugging Face
**Independent researcher · Sept 3 · 1 day ago**

An independent researcher spent three weeks scraping TikTok's private Android API, pulling 5.94 billion videos and 3.23 billion creator profiles, then published 4.5 billion video records — captions, engagement counts, sound, country, posting time — as a 289GB dataset on Hugging Face, for $950 a month. The dataset's own card admits the captions count as GDPR-protected personal data and says that obligation transfers to whoever downloads it.

*Why it matters:* a scrape costing less than a laptop produced a dataset with continent-spanning privacy exposure, and nothing currently stops it from happening again.

## 5. Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max-0902 Tops the CodeArena Coding Leaderboard
**Alibaba · Sept 2 · 2 days ago**

Alibaba's updated Qwen3.8-Max-0902 gained 22 points on the CodeArena WebDev leaderboard to reach 1,691, edging past Claude Opus 5 Max (1,687) and Kimi K3 Max (1,674) for the top spot, while keeping its 1-million-token context window.

*Why it matters:* the top coding-leaderboard spot changed hands again within days — a sign of how compressed frontier-lab release cycles have become.
